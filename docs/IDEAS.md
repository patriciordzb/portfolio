# Ideas

Running list of ideas for the portfolio — content, features, design. No commitment, just capture.

## Blog Post Ideas

1. **"Why LPBF Gas Flow Is Harder to Measure Than You Think"**
   - Discuss the engineering challenge of measuring gas flow in a sealed, hot, laser-active chamber
   - Connect to the America Makes standards work
   
2. **"ANOVA for Engineers Who Haven't Taken Stats Since Freshman Year"**
   - Practical explainer on one-way ANOVA using real GRX-810 tensile data as the example
   - Intended audience: engineers who need to analyze multi-condition tensile datasets

3. **"How to Supervise a Lab Team Without Being a Manager"**
   - Lessons from supervising undergrads through metallography and AM workflows
   - What works, what doesn't, how to give feedback to junior engineers

4. **"Building TaskFlow: What I Wish I Knew Before Picking Supabase"**
   - Technical reflection on the stack choices and what I'd do differently
   - Real-time subscriptions, auth, edge functions

5. **"The SHPE/MAES Leadership Playbook: How to Run Academic Programming for Engineers"**
   - Director of Academics experience — proposals, events, program design, onboarding

6. **"What It's Actually Like to Train 400 DoD Personnel in AM"**
   - Reflections on the DRIVE AM Foundation experience
   - What military engineers want to know vs. what industry trainers assume they want to know

7. **"From Die-Cast to LPBF: What Automotive Engineers Ask in Every Meeting"**
   - Common questions and concerns from DRIVE AM Youngstown sessions
   - Practical guide to the die-cast → AM transition conversation
